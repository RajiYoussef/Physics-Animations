# Ideal Conductor

This module explores the electrostatic properties
of ideal conductors through physics animations.

## Physical Principles

- The electric field inside an ideal conductor
  is zero in electrostatic equilibrium.
- Electric potential is constant throughout
  the conductor.
- Excess charge resides on its surface.
- The electric field immediately outside
  the surface is perpendicular to it.

## Objectives

- Visualize electric fields around conductors.
- Explore equipotential surfaces.
- Illustrate surface charge distributions.
- Study electrostatic boundary conditions.
